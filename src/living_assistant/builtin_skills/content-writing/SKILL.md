# Content writing

## When to use
A task asks for written content: a blog post, social posts, web copy, a script, a newsletter.

## Steps
1. Confirm the audience, the goal (inform, persuade, sell), the length and the tone from the task.
2. Outline: hook, 3-5 main points, conclusion with one call to action.
3. Write plainly: short sentences, concrete examples, no filler.
4. Save the result in the requested file and format.

## Rules and checks
- Facts and numbers must come from the provided research or a cited source.
- No invented quotes, testimonials or statistics.
